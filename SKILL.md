# Channel Plan

Made by Built Young (builtyoung.org). This credit is for people reading the skill. Never add it to a founder's content.

A small team that posts everywhere gets nowhere. This skill picks three channels the team can keep up for a month, with a reason for each, and says what to post there. Built for Built Young classes and for any first-time founder.

## Workflow

### 1. Intake (ask only for what's missing)

- Who uses the product and who pays, with age, situation, region and language
- Where those people already spend time, and how the team knows
- Hours per week the team can give to marketing, in total
- Unique access: anything the team can reach that a stranger can't (they run a club, a parent owns a shop, they're active in a community, they have a following)
- What they can make: text, posters, phone video, a founder on camera
- Founders' age bracket

If the user pastes a `GTM PIPELINE` or `GTM PROFILE` block, take these from it.

### 2. List the candidates

Start from this list and add anything specific to the audience:

- **Short video:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, RED (Xiaohongshu)
- **Feeds and communities:** Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Discord servers, WeChat or WhatsApp groups
- **Direct:** cold email, DMs to people who asked for this, partner organisations
- **In person:** school clubs, assemblies, local events, posters with a QR code, a stall

### 3. Score each one

Score 1 to 3 on four things, 12 in total, and fill the evidence column for Reach. A guess gets a 1.

| Channel | Reach: are they really there? | Evidence | Cost (free = 3) | Time (few hours = 3) | Unique access | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Time is honest hours: one short video is one to two hours to plan, shoot and edit; three a week is half a day.

### 4. Pick three

Take the top scores, then check the set against these, and swap if it fails one:

- At least one channel uses the team's unique access. It is usually the one that converts.
- If the payer is not the user, at least one channel reaches the payer.
- At most one high-effort channel (short video) for a team of one to three.
- The three together fit inside the team's weekly hours.

Name one channel you rejected and why. A channel that scored low is not a bad channel; it is the wrong one for this audience right now.

### 5. Check the platform's rules

Before a founder under 18 opens an account, check that platform's own pages for its minimum age and any parent-consent rule. The terms of service is the rule; the platform's own help centre is acceptable when it states the age plainly, and is often the only page that opens. Say what you found, which kind of page it was, the link and the date you read it. Never take an age rule from a blog or a third-party list. If neither page opens, write `[NEEDS: age rule for <platform>]` and don't plan an account there on a guess. Under 13: no personal social accounts; use in-person channels and accounts run by a parent or the program.

### 6. Set the content pillars

Three pillars, each one a kind of post the team can make every week without running dry. A good set covers: the problem the audience feels, the product in use, and the team's own story. For each pillar give two post ideas per chosen channel, in the format that channel rewards.

### 7. Deliver

- The scoring sheet
- The three channels, one reason each, and the one rejected
- The three pillars with first post ideas
- A posting rhythm the hours allow (three posts per channel per week is the class default; fewer is fine if it is kept up)
- A reminder that every channel needs its own tracked link (the landing-waitlist skill makes them)

End with **For the run record:** `Channels: [three]` and `Content pillars: [three]`.

## Rules

- Reach is scored on evidence: a named group, a search that shows the audience asking, the team's own contacts. "Everyone is on TikTok" is not evidence that the payer is.
- Never recommend buying followers, engagement pods, mass DMs or scraping.
- Posting in someone else's group needs that group's rules read and its admin asked.
- Don't promise results from a channel. Say what it would take to find out: three weeks of posts with tracked links.

## Class mode

For several teams, return each team's scoring sheet and three picks, and one line each team says aloud: "We picked A, B and C because ..., and our unique access is ...".

## Language

Reply in the user's language. Post ideas are written in the language of each channel's audience.
